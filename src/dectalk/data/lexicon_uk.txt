# UK-English overrides on top of the bundled US lexicon.
#
# This file lists the words that pronounce differently in Received
# Pronunciation (RP) than in General American. The full DECtalk Dic_uk.txt
# is proprietary FONIX and cannot be redistributed; this is a small
# starter set of the most common spelling and pronunciation differences.
#
# Format matches the US lexicon: WORD whitespace ARPABET-PHONEMES.

# r-loss in non-rhotic accents (RP doesn't pronounce r before consonants
# or at word end; we approximate by removing the R phoneme). The
# bundled US lexicon includes the R; UK overrides drop it.
